Chivayo Beneficiaries

Project Overview

The Database is a non commercial data project dedicated to cataloging the documented resource distributions made by Mr Wicknell Chivayo. Beginning in 2023, Mr. Chivayo has been documented as distributing significant resources including vehicles, direct financial grants, and institutional support across various sectors of Zimbabwean society. This archive aggregates data from verified media reports to provide a centralized overview of these transactions.

Mission

To maintain a factual, non partisan record of public benefaction for the purposes of historical documentation, media reference, and public knowledge.

Methodology

All information is sourced from public news outlets. We note that values are estimated based on market rates at the time of reporting, and that some gifts may have been subsequently declined or altered.

All information is based on publicly available media reports. Please verify independently.
